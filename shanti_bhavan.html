Shanti Bhavan - Aged Home, Kalyan

A place of peace and dignity for our Seniors, since 1973.

History & Formation

Shanti Bhavan was instituted in the year 1973, and is a 32-bed facility caring for the aged in Kalyan.

The Sisters of the Congregation of Holy Mount Rosary (HMR) take care of the Seniors at the home. The SSVP members, under its Chairperson and MC members, look after the administration and management of the Home.
