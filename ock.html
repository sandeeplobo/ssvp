Ozanam Community Kitchen (OCK)

Feeding the hungry with dignity at Mother Velankanni Church, Cheetah Camp, Trombay.

History & Formation

The Ozanam Community Kitchen (OCK) was instituted and blessed on 27th September 2009, by His Lordship Agnelo Gracias at the Mother Velankanni Church, Cheetah Camp, Trombay, Mumbai.

The program feeds one square meal a day to approximately 100 persons, a commitment it has faithfully upheld for the last 17 years.

The CCPS (Precious Blood of Jesus) Religious congregation runs this Parish and has been very supportive of this CCB project.
