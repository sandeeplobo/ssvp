Central Councils

The 84 regional pillars guiding our mission across India.

Role & Function

According to the Rule of the Society, Central Councils are instituted at the Diocesan level. They are formed by grouping together Area Councils within a specific diocese.

Their primary purpose is to maintain a link between the Conferences and the National Council, representing the Society in matters of local importance and animating the activity of Area Councils and Conferences under their jurisdiction.
