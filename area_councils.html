Area Councils

The 8 regional pillars (Deanaries) guiding our mission across Mumbai and suburbs.

Role & Function

According to the Rule of the Society, Area Councils are instituted at the Deanery level. They are formed by grouping together several societies within a specific deanery.

Their primary purpose is to maintain a link between the Conferences and the Central Council, representing the Society in matters of local importance and animating the activity of the Conferences under their jurisdiction.
