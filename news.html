13th September, 2026

Shanti Bhavan Annual General Meeting 2026

Residents, Sisters and the Managing Committee of Shanti Bhavan, Kalyan came together for the Annual General Meeting.

Shanti Bhavan, our 32-bed home for the aged in Kalyan, held its Annual General Meeting for 2026. The home has been caring for Seniors since 1973, with the Sisters of the Congregation of Holy Mount Rosary looking after the residents and SSVP members managing its administration.
The holy mass was celebrated by Rev. Father Simon D'souza Parish Priest of our Lady of Lourdes Church, Kalyan West.

Photos from the AGM are now available in the Shanti Bhavan photo gallery.
